# Remote Sensing VLM Paper Reader

Build an evidence-linked understanding of each paper and update durable research knowledge without erasing how earlier judgments changed.

## Boundary

Use this Skill for remote-sensing VLMs, bi-temporal vision-language reasoning, change captioning, semantic change detection, and closely related multimodal change-detection research. Do not silently generalize its domain framework to unrelated medical, social-science, or generic PDF summarization tasks.

The installed Plugin is reusable. PDFs, notes, research ideas, and ledgers are private user data and belong in a user-chosen research library, never inside the Plugin.

## Select the mode

- **Quick screening:** decide whether full reading is worthwhile.
- **Beginner deep reading:** teach prerequisites before formal details.
- **Reproduction analysis:** inspect implementation, data, training, compute, and missing details.
- **Innovation analysis:** test novelty assumptions and identify decisive experiments.
- **Cross-paper comparison:** reconcile papers that affect the same idea, module, or decision.

Read [references/deep-reading-protocol.md](references/deep-reading-protocol.md) for the selected mode. Read [references/remote-sensing-vlm-framework.md](references/remote-sensing-vlm-framework.md) when placing or comparing methods. Read the note, ledger, and update references only when producing or modifying those artifacts.

## Acquire evidence

When Paper Pilot tools are available, use them for search, retrieval, consecutive full-text reading, and decisive-page rendering. Follow continuations needed for the relevant full-paper read and inspect central figures and tables.

When Paper Pilot is unavailable, read a user-provided local PDF and state that online search and retrieval were not performed. Never present an abstract-only analysis as a full-paper reading. If paper identity, version, or intended reading goal is materially ambiguous, ask one focused question.

## Analyze and write

Separate three labels throughout: **author claim**, **evidence supports**, and **analysis/inference**. Cite PDF pages for method, figure, result, and limitation claims. For equations, explain symbols, tensor shapes, operations, motivation, then a small example when useful.

Before writing, determine the research-library path. If a destination exists, is locked, or has unclear provenance, stop and report the exact path; do not overwrite it.

For a Word note, follow [references/paper-note-schema.md](references/paper-note-schema.md). For Excel updates, follow [references/research-ledger-schema.md](references/research-ledger-schema.md) and [references/update-policy.md](references/update-policy.md).

## Preserve evolving knowledge

Compare each new paper against active ideas and methods. Record duplicate, partially overlapping, covered, contradicted, superseded, adopted, or archived relationships with evidence. Change status rather than hard-deleting a row. End by reporting what was added, revised, downgraded, archived, and left unresolved.
